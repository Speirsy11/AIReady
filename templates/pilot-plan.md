## Scope

In scope:

- draft-only output;
- fictional/sample or explicitly approved non-sensitive data;
- human review before any use;
- local notes and checklists.

Out of scope:

- production automation;
- customer-facing output without review;
- secrets, credentials, private customer data, regulated data;
- account changes, spend, outreach, publishing, or deployment.

## Approval gates

Before proceeding beyond draft-only review, require explicit approval for:

- real workflow data;
- private/customer data;
- tool/account access;
- automation;
- external communication;
- deployment or publication.
