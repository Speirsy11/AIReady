## Working repo title

AI Adoption Readiness Kit

## Short description

Local-first templates for small teams to assess safe, measurable AI adoption candidates before any pilot.

## Longer description

A fictional-data starter kit for reviewing whether a small team is ready to adopt AI safely. It includes a readiness questionnaire, use-case scorecard, scoring rubric, draft-only pilot plan, fictional examples, and local review/preflight docs. The kit is designed to surface blockers before anyone touches private systems, customer data, production workflows, outreach, deployment, spend, or account changes.

## Audience

- small teams considering practical AI use cases;
- solo consultants preparing a safe discovery/review process;
- technical reviewers who need an approval-gated pilot plan;
- founders/operators who want to separate useful internal AI workflows from risky customer-impacting automation.

## What this is

- A local-only readiness review template set.
- A way to prioritize AI use cases by value, repetition, data safety, human review, and measurability.
- A guardrail-heavy process for marking risky workflows `[blocked]` before any real data or systems are used.
- A possible future starting point for a workshop, self-assessment, or paid workflow audit entry point, only after explicit approval.

## What this is not

- Not a SaaS product.
- Not legal, financial, compliance, health, or security advice.
- Not permission to process customer data or regulated data.
- Not permission to inspect private systems, production workflows, accounts, credentials, or private repositories.
- Not permission to deploy, automate, contact users/customers, publish, push, spend money, or create payment flows.
- Not a claim that AI outputs are safe to use without human review.
