# Service Note — AI Adoption Readiness Kit

This is a local product/service positioning note, not a public offer.

## Possible buyer

Small teams considering AI tools but unsure where adoption is safe, useful, and measurable.

## Problem

Teams often jump from "AI could help" to tool trials without mapping data sensitivity, human review, approval gates, and measurable pilot outcomes.

## Local offer shape

A small readiness review could produce:

- safe workflow intake;
- scored AI use-case shortlist;
- blocked/risky use-case notes;
- draft-only pilot plan;
- approval gates and stop conditions.

## Non-goals

- No autonomous deployment.
- No private-system inspection without approval.
- No customer data processing by default.
- No claims of compliance, security certification, revenue uplift, or guaranteed time savings.
- No outreach or paid offer until Charlie explicitly approves.
