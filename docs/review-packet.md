## Review order

## Go / no-go criteria

### Continue locally if

- The kit helps choose safe, measurable AI use cases.
- Blocked workflows are obvious before any real data is used.
- A reviewer can understand the assessment without private systems.
- The pilot plan stays draft-only and human-reviewed.

### Stop or rethink if

- Value depends on private/customer data before approval.
- The kit encourages deployment or automation too early.
- Success criteria are vague or impossible to measure.
- Skipped checks are hidden rather than marked `not reviewed` or `[blocked]`.
